# Moodboard rules

`/moodboard` shows how the system looks. The values live in [`src/tokens/tokens.ts`](src/tokens/tokens.ts) and the shared components in [`src/ui/`](src/ui/index.tsx). Read this file before writing any UI. The decision is recorded in [ADR 0001](docs/adr/0001-moodboard-is-the-only-ui-source.md).

## Rules

1. **Only tokens.** Colors, fonts, font sizes, spacing, radii and shadows come from CSS variables (`var(--color-accent)`, `var(--space-4)` and so on). Never write a hex, `rgb()`, a font name, or a media query outside `src/tokens/`. `npm run check:tokens` enforces this.
2. **Only shared components.** Build from `Button`, `Card`, `Badge`, `Field`, `Grid`, `Col`, `ProductImage`, `QuantityStepper`, `DataRow`, `ChoiceGroup`, `ChoiceCard`, `Hero`, `Accordion`, `Quote`, `ProgressBar`, `NavTabs`, and the storefront blocks `IconCircle`, `PromoStrip`, `UtilityBar`, `SiteHeader`, `CategoryNav`, `Carousel` (with `BannerSlide`), `ShortcutRow`/`ShortcutCard`, `AppPromoCard`, and the sale blocks `SectionHeader` (with `Countdown`), `FlashSaleCard`, `TileMosaic` and `BrandChip`, and the content blocks `ArticleCard`, `BenefitBar` and `SiteFooter`, and `MegaMenu` (the three-level category panel; a top-level node's `image` fills the circles of its leaves), and the product-page blocks `Breadcrumb`, `ProductGallery`, `Tabs` and `StickyActionBar`, and the form blocks `StepPill`, `Select`, `Textarea` (for text over several lines), `Switch`, `Checkbox` and `Notice`, and the account blocks `SettingRow`, `AccountSidebar`, `MemberBanner`, `StatCard` and `BottomTabBar`, the cart block `CartItem`, the product card `ProductCard`, the menu blocks `DropdownMenu` and `BranchCard`, the article blocks `ArticleBody` and `ShareBar`, the order blocks `OrderCard`, `OrderTimeline` and `StatusHeader`, a sign-in card built from `Card`, `Button`, `Tabs` and `Field`, and the policy page (a contents card of ghost `Button`s beside a `Card` of numbered sections), and the coupon ticket `CouponCard` (opens a `Modal` with the details), and the club page (a member card and tier cards built from `Card`, `Badge`, `DataRow` and `MemberBanner`, with points coupons as `CouponCard`), and the rating stars `RatingStars` (show a score or let the shopper pick one), the wallet card `BalanceCard`, and the service card and booking card (built from `Card`, `IconCircle`, `Badge`, `DataRow` and `Button`), and the not-found block (a large code, a message and two buttons), and the cookie notice (a floating `Card` with three `Button`s, and a `Modal` of `Switch`es for the settings), and the panel `Modal` (a native dialog over the page: sign-in and register open in it from the header), and the company landing page blocks `SiteNav` (a top bar with its menu behind a button on a phone, see-through over the opening band with `overlay`), `Hero` with `tone="on-dark"` (a full-width accent band with `lead` and `media`), `LogoWall` (plain client-name tiles, never real logos), `FeatureShowcase` (a product pitch beside its picture), `CourseCard` and `ContactFooter` (a company foot), and the category blocks `CategoryCard`, `CategoryTile` (also with the name over the picture), `CategoryPills` and `FilterGroup`. Icons come from the shared `Icon` (Lucide, `src/ui/icon.tsx`) by name; put them in an `IconCircle` with its `icon` prop, and leave the circle empty where the icon is not decided. Never draw or paste your own SVG, never set an icon colour, and give icon-only buttons a label. Use the `row__grow` class to push the next item in a `row` to the far end. If a Prototype needs something else (a price table, a video player), propose it to the user first. Add it to `src/ui/` and `/moodboard` only after they approve.
3. **One layout container.** Pages use `Grid` and `Col`: 4 columns on phones, 8 from `sm`, 12 from `md`. Do not write custom breakpoints; change the numbers in `tokens.breakpoint` if they ever need to move.
4. **Both languages.** Every visible string is a `{ th, en }` pair. Prototype content lives in a Dataset, never in the component.
5. **Responsive from the start.** Check every page on a phone width and a laptop width.
6. **Rows line up.** When cards sit side by side, the same part of each card (price, button, title) must land at the same height whatever the text length: pin the footer to the bottom with `margin-top: auto` and let the grid make the row one height. Product grids always use `ProductCard`; never build a product card by hand.
7. **Add to cart looks the same everywhere.** Every add-to-cart button is `<Button icon="cart">`: the cart icon before the label, in the label's colour.
8. **Product pictures are square.** A picture of a product (card, cart line, product page) is 1:1, shown with `<ProductImage square />`, and one 1000×1000px file serves them all. Pictures of articles and categories keep the 4:3 frame, except an article cover that carries its headline (`ArticleCard` with `image`, and the cover on the article page), which is 3:2 so the text is not cropped.
9. **Brand pictures come in as props.** Third-party badges and logos (App Store, Google Play, payment and social logos, and popular-brand logos in `src/assets/brand/brands/`) live in `src/assets/brand/` and reach `SiteFooter` and `AppPromoCard` through their props; never place a logo file inside a component or a page. They are other companies' trademarks, used only so the prototype looks finished.
    Category pictures work the same way: resized copies live in `src/assets/categories/` and `categoryImage(label)` finds one by the Thai category name, so a page passes it to `ProductImage` as `src`; a category without a picture keeps the letter frame. Keep the full-size originals out of the app (they are git-ignored in `src/assets/brand/Categories/`).
10. **A card that leads somewhere reacts and is clickable as a whole.** `ProductCard`, a `Card` with `to`, the shortcut, flash-sale, article and category cards all take the shared hover and keyboard-focus state (accent border thickened inward, accent title) from `storefront.css`, and the whole card is the click target: the name or the read / shop button stretches its link over the card while real buttons stay on top. Do not write a hover style per page, and keep the ring inside the card because carousels clip anything outside it.
11. **Advertising banners can be pictures.** `BannerSlide` and `PromoStrip` take an optional `image`: a finished advertising picture that already carries its Thai headline. Pictures live as resized copies in `src/assets/banners/` and are chosen per dataset and used only on the Thai page, so the English page and a dataset without pictures keep the plain text banners. The headline stays in the page as hidden text for screen readers. Keep the full-size originals out of the app (git-ignored in `src/assets/brand/slide-banner/` and `promo-strip/`).
12. **Shop-by-job tiles can carry a picture.** `TileMosaic` takes `{ label, src? }`: a tile with `src` fills with the picture and puts its label on a dark band (the same band as the overlay category tile); a tile without keeps the letter frame. Pictures are resized copies in `src/assets/jobs/`, found by the Thai job name with `jobImage(job)`; the full-size originals stay out of the app (git-ignored in `src/assets/brand/Shop by job/`).
13. **The Landing page has its own theme.** Everything in the Landing prototype sits inside `.theme-landing`, which swaps the colours (a brick-red accent on a white page) and the font (DM Sans) for the values in `landingTheme` in `src/tokens/tokens.ts`; the extra colours of that page (the sky of the opening band, its highlighted words, the soft blue behind the FAQ, the dark foot) are tokens too. No other prototype uses the theme, and nothing inside the Landing page may use a colour outside the two token sets. Shown on `/moodboard` as 1.4.

## Color roles

| Token | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `bg` | Page background |
| `surface` | Cards, forms, bars |
| `surfaceAlt` | Secondary or subtly separated areas |
| `border` | Borders and dividers |
| `text` / `textMuted` | Primary and secondary text |
| `accent` / `accentHover` / `accentText` | Primary actions and links; one accent action per screen |
| `accentSoft` | Soft highlights such as badges and selected items |
| `success` / `warning` / `danger` | Status; `danger` also marks destructive actions |

## Starting direction

The current values are a neutral starting point: warm off-white surfaces, an indigo accent, Inter with Noto Sans Thai, and generous rounded corners. Change `tokens.ts` and the whole system follows.
